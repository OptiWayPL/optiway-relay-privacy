The “OptiWay Relay” extension (the “extension”) lets you transfer a transport order from the Amazon Relay platform to the OptiwayTMS system with one click, and marks which loads are already in OptiwayTMS. This policy describes what data the extension processes, for what purpose, and with whom it is shared.

1. Purpose of the extension (single purpose)

The extension has a single purpose: transferring order data from Amazon Relay to OptiwayTMS and indicating which loads are already present in OptiwayTMS. The extension does not serve advertising, behavioral tracking, or profiling.

2. What data we process

a) OptiwayTMS sign-in credentials. The email address and password you enter in the extension panel are used solely to sign you in to your OptiwayTMS account (authentication). The password is not stored by the extension — it is used once to obtain a session token.

b) Session tokens. After sign-in the extension stores session tokens (access and refresh) only within the extension's protected service-worker context (not accessible to web pages, not written into page content). They are used to authorize writing orders to OptiwayTMS.

c) Order data from Amazon Relay. When you use the extension on the Amazon Relay site, it reads the data of the visible load (e.g. load identifier, order type, pickup/delivery locations and times) and — upon your explicit action (clicking “Send to TMS”) — sends it to OptiwayTMS as a new order. The extension also reads which loads already exist in OptiwayTMS in order to mark them.

d) Diagnostic data (errors). In case of an error, the extension may send to a monitoring service (Sentry) a limited, redacted error message from a fixed list of codes, plus an opaque identifier (UUID) of your account for correlation. We do not send your email address or raw data from the Amazon Relay page. Error monitoring can be disabled (no DSN configured).

3. Who we share data with (recipients)

Data is sent only to the infrastructure serving OptiwayTMS and to the monitoring processor:

  • OptiwayTMS (application backend) — your destination system, where the order is saved.
  • Authentication service (self-hosted Supabase) — verifies sign-in and issues the session token.
  • Sentry — error monitoring (only redacted error codes + UUID, as in point 2d).

We do not sell data, do not share it with data brokers or advertisers, and do not use it for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose (point 1).

4. Storage and deletion

The extension itself does not permanently store any data — it is a transfer tool.

  • Sign-in credentials and session tokens — stored locally in the browser only for the duration of the active session; signing out or uninstalling the extension removes them from the device. No permanent retention on the extension side.
  • Order data — once sent, it resides in OptiwayTMS (not in the extension) and is subject to OptiwayTMS retention rules: 5 years.
  • Diagnostic data (Sentry) — redacted error codes + UUID, retained according to Sentry's retention policy for 30 days.
